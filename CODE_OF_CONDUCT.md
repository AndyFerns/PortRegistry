# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0). The text below is a short adaptation of it.

## Our pledge

We want participation in PortRegistry to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to the tooling;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- owning mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and the community, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other place where the project is discussed) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That can include removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning a contributor.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, not in a public issue:

- Use GitHub's **private reporting** on this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability", and say that it is a conduct report), or
- contact the maintainer, [@AndyFerns](https://github.com/AndyFerns), through the contact details on their GitHub profile.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in order of severity:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning with clarity about the violation, for inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from the project's spaces for serious violations or sustained inappropriate behaviour.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.
